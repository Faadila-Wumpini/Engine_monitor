How it works

Four layers. One goal — catch the problem before it breaks your engine.

01
⚡
Sense

Two sensors on the engine block read vibration on 3 axes and surface temperature, 50 times per second.

MPU6050 · DS18B20
02
📡
Transmit

The ESP32 microcontroller packages sensor data as JSON and streams it straight to the receiving device over an encrypted Bluetooth Low Energy connection — no Wi-Fi network required.

ESP32 · Bluetooth Low Energy · GATT
03
🧠
Analyse

Python server filters the signal, extracts 22 features, and runs Isolation Forest inference to score each window.

Isolation Forest · FFT · Butterworth
04
🔔
Alert

If the score drops below the threshold, a severity-graded alert fires and the event is logged to the cloud.

Streamlit · Supabase
// sensors

What it measures

Two sensors. Five numbers per cycle. Everything you need to know about your engine's health in real time.

SENSOR 01 · MPU6050 ACCELEROMETER

Vibration

Measures how much the engine is shaking in three directions simultaneously — left/right, front/back, and up/down. Abnormal vibration is the earliest warning sign of misfires, worn bearings, and mechanical imbalance.

X axisLeft / Right
Y axisFront / Back
Z axisUp / Down
Sample rate50 Hz
Resolution16-bit ADC
InterfaceI2C (GPIO 21/22)
SENSOR 02 · DS18B20 TEMPERATURE

Temperature

Monitors the surface temperature of the engine block every second. Rising temperature indicates cooling system failure, oil starvation, or excessive friction — all causes of catastrophic engine damage if undetected.

Range-10°C to 85°C
Accuracy± 0.5°C
Resolution12-bit
Sample rate1 Hz
Interface1-Wire (GPIO 4)
Form factorWaterproof probe
